# Truth-and-Shadows

Truth and Shadows is a poetic 3D puzzle-platformer about navigating the fragmented depths of your own mind. Shift between body and shadow to solve light- and darkness-based puzzles in a surreal desert world shaped by memory and emotion. As the world changes, so does the meaning of the story you uncover—told line by line through a reversible poem. Face what was buried. Choose who you become.

play the game at: https://iexploze.itch.io/truth-and-shadows
